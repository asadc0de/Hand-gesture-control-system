Gesture to Function Mapping (Ordered from Simple to Complex)

============================================================
LEVEL 1: BASIC POINTER & CURSOR
============================================================

1. MOVE POINTER (Cursor Movement)
   Gesture: Right hand, index finger extended, others folded.
   Action: Moves the screen cursor smoothly based on index fingertip tracking.


============================================================
LEVEL 2: MOUSE CLICKS & DRAGGING
============================================================

2. LEFT CLICK (Left Click)
   Gesture: Left hand, Thumb + Middle finger pinch (quick tap). Fires immediately on tap release.

3. DOUBLE CLICK (Double Click)
   Gesture: Left hand, two quick taps of Thumb + Middle finger pinch in rapid succession.

4. RIGHT CLICK (Right Click)
   Gesture: Left hand, Thumb + Ring finger pinch (quick tap). Fires immediately on tap release.

5. DRAG (Mouse Click & Drag)
   Gesture: Left hand, Thumb + Middle finger sustained pinch (hold > 250ms). Release to drop.


============================================================
LEVEL 3: SCROLLING & NAVIGATION
============================================================

6. SCROLL (Scroll)
   Gesture: Index and middle extended, others folded ("Peace" sign / V sign). Move hand up/down to scroll.

7. BACK (Navigate Back)
   Gesture: Only thumb extended, others folded (Thumbs up).

8. FORWARD (Navigate Forward)
   Gesture: Only pinky extended, others folded.


============================================================
LEVEL 4: WINDOW MANAGEMENT (SINGLE-HAND)
============================================================

9. MINIMIZE (Minimize Active Window)
   Gesture: All five fingers folded (Fist).

10. RESTORE_ACTIVE (Restore Active Window)
    Gesture: All five fingers fully extended (Flat palm / high five).

11. CLOSE (Close Window)
    Gesture: Thumb and pinky extended, others folded ("Shaka" sign / Hang loose).

12. SCREENSHOT (Take a Screenshot)
    Gesture: Index, pinky, and thumb extended, others folded ("Spiderman" or "I love you" sign).


============================================================
LEVEL 5: SPECIALIZED MODES & CONTROLS
============================================================

13. TOGGLE_PRESENTATION_MODE (Toggle Presentation Mode ON/OFF)
    Gesture: Index, middle, and ring fingers extended, others folded (Three fingers up).

14. NEXT SLIDE / PREV SLIDE (Swipe Right / Swipe Left)
    Gesture: While in Presentation Mode, flat palm (all fingers extended), move hand horizontally.

15. ZOOM IN / ZOOM OUT (Pinch to Zoom)
    Gesture: While in Presentation Mode, fold middle, ring, and pinky fingers. Move thumb and index closer (Zoom OUT) or spread them apart (Zoom IN).

16. TOGGLE_MEDIA_MODE (Toggle Media Mode ON/OFF)
    Gesture: Index, middle, ring, and pinky fingers extended, thumb folded (4-finger salute).
    Action: Activates or deactivates Media Mode for volume/brightness control.

17. VOLUME CONTROL (Adjust System Volume)
    Gesture: Left hand, pinch index and thumb. Hand must be VERTICAL (fingers pointing up). Media Mode MUST be active.

18. BRIGHTNESS CONTROL (Adjust Screen Brightness)
    Gesture: Left hand, pinch index and thumb. Hand must be HORIZONTAL (fingers pointing sideways). Media Mode MUST be active.


============================================================
LEVEL 6: MULTI-HAND & SYSTEM CONTROLS
============================================================

19. MINIMIZE_ALL (Minimize All Windows)
    Gesture: Two hands, both forming a Fist.

20. RESTORE_ALL (Restore All Windows)
    Gesture: Two hands, both open (Not fists) - only works if you just used Minimize All.

21. RESTORE_LAST (Restore Last Minimized Window)
    Gesture: One hand fist, one hand open (or single hand fist while in window management mode).

22. LOCK (Lock Laptop)
    Gesture: Two hands, index finger only on each, crossed together to form an X.
