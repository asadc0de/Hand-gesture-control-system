Gesture to Function Mapping

1. SCROLL (Scroll)
   Gesture: Index and middle extended, others folded ("Peace" sign / V sign).

2. MINIMIZE (Minimize Active Window)
   Gesture: All five fingers folded (Fist).

3. RESTORE_ACTIVE (Restore Active Window)
   Gesture: All five fingers fully extended (Flat palm / high five).

4. CLOSE (Close Window)
   Gesture: Thumb and pinky extended, others folded ("Shaka" sign / Hang loose).

5. BACK (Navigate Back)
   Gesture: Only thumb extended, others folded (Thumbs up).

6. FORWARD (Navigate Forward)
   Gesture: Only pinky extended, others folded.

7. MINIMIZE_ALL (Minimize All Windows)
   Gesture: Two hands, both forming a Fist.

8. RESTORE_ALL (Restore All Windows)
   Gesture: Two hands, both open (Not fists) - only works if you just used Minimize All.

9. RESTORE_LAST (Restore Last Minimized Window)
   Gesture: One hand fist, one hand open (or single hand fist while in window management mode).

10. TOGGLE_PRESENTATION_MODE (Toggle Presentation Mode ON/OFF)
    Gesture: Index, middle, and ring fingers extended, others folded (Three fingers up).

11. SCREENSHOT (Take a Screenshot)
    Gesture: Index, pinky, and thumb extended, others folded ("Spiderman" or "I love you" sign).

12. LOCK (Lock Laptop)
    Gesture: Two hands, index finger only on each, crossed together to form an X.

---- While in Presentation Mode ----

13. NEXT SLIDE / PREV SLIDE (Swipe Right / Swipe Left)
    Gesture: Flat palm (all fingers extended), move hand horizontally.

14. ZOOM IN / ZOOM OUT (Pinch to Zoom)
    Gesture: Trigger a DRAG gesture (hold index finger extended), then move thumb and index closer or further apart.

15. TOGGLE_MEDIA_MODE (Toggle Media Mode)
    Gesture: Index, middle, ring, and pinky fingers extended, thumb folded (4-finger salute).
    Action: Activates or deactivates Media Mode for volume/brightness control.

---- Left Hand Controls ----

16. VOLUME CONTROL (Adjust System Volume)
    Gesture: Left hand, pinch index and thumb. Hand must be VERTICAL (fingers pointing up). Media Mode MUST be active.

17. BRIGHTNESS CONTROL (Adjust Screen Brightness)
    Gesture: Left hand, pinch index and thumb. Hand must be HORIZONTAL (fingers pointing sideways). Media Mode MUST be active.

18. LEFT CLICK (Left Click)
    Gesture: Thumb + Middle finger pinch (quick tap). Fires immediately on tap release.

19. DOUBLE CLICK (Double Click)
    Gesture: Two quick taps of Thumb + Middle finger pinch in rapid succession.

20. DRAG (Mouse Click & Drag)
    Gesture: Thumb + Middle finger sustained pinch (hold > 250ms). Release to drop.

21. RIGHT CLICK (Right Click)
    Gesture: Thumb + Ring finger pinch (quick tap). Fires immediately on tap release.
