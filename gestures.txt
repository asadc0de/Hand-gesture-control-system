Gesture to Function Mapping

1. MOVE (Move Mouse Cursor)
   Gesture: Index finger extended, middle and thumb folded (Pointing).

2. CLICK (Left Click)
   Gesture: Index and thumb extended, others folded ("L" shape / finger gun).
   Note: Holding this gesture for a few frames triggers DRAG.

3. DOUBLE_CLICK (Double Click)
   Gesture: Index, middle, and thumb extended, others folded.

4. RIGHT_CLICK (Right Click)
   Gesture: Index and pinky extended, others folded (Rock sign / horns).

5. DRAG (Click and Drag)
   Gesture: Same as CLICK ("L" shape), but held for several frames.

6. SCROLL (Scroll)
   Gesture: Index and middle extended, ring folded ("Peace" sign / V sign).

7. MINIMIZE (Minimize Active Window)
   Gesture: All five fingers folded (Fist).

8. RESTORE_ACTIVE (Restore Active Window)
   Gesture: All five fingers fully extended (Flat palm / high five).

9. CLOSE (Close Window)
   Gesture: Thumb and pinky extended, others folded ("Shaka" sign / Hang loose).

10. BACK (Navigate Back)
    Gesture: Only thumb extended, others folded (Thumbs up).

11. FORWARD (Navigate Forward)
    Gesture: Only pinky extended, others folded.

12. MINIMIZE_ALL (Minimize All Windows)
    Gesture: Two hands, both forming a Fist.

13. RESTORE_ALL (Restore All Windows)
    Gesture: Two hands, both open (Not fists) - only works if you just used Minimize All.

14. RESTORE_LAST (Restore Last Minimized Window)
    Gesture: One hand fist, one hand open (or single hand fist while in window management mode).
